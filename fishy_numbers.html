📚 給家長的話 · For Parents

🎯 學習重點 · Learning Focus

數字序列考的是發現規律：2、4、6、_、10，下個是多少？順數、倒數、隔位跳數，全部都是邏輯推理加數感的綜合運用，同時為之後學乘數表鋪路。小貓釣魚的情境，讓「找規律」變成一場尋寶遊戲。

Number sequences are about spotting the rule: 2, 4, 6, _, 10 — what comes next? Counting forwards, backwards, and skipping all combine logical reasoning with number sense, while paving the way for times tables later. The kitten-fishing theme turns pattern-hunting into a treasure hunt.

👨‍👩‍👧 陪玩小貼士 · Playing Together

重點不是答對，而是說得出「為什麼」：「因為每次多2。」請小朋友指著相鄰兩個數，算一算它們之間的差，規律自然浮現。說得出規律，才是真正理解。

The goal isn't the right answer — it's saying why: "because it goes up by 2 each time." Ask your child to point at two neighbours and find the difference; the pattern reveals itself. Being able to state the rule is real understanding.

🏠 生活延伸練習 · Everyday Practice

等巴士時看門牌（單雙數排列）、看日曆（每行加7）、數電梯樓層 — 生活周圍都是數列。這個階段可以讓小朋友玩百數表（1-100格子表），對數字規律的感覺會突飛猛進。

Look at door numbers (odds and evens) while waiting for the bus, the calendar (each row adds 7), lift floors — sequences are everywhere. A hundred chart (1–100 grid) is a great toy at this stage; your child's feel for number patterns will leap forward.
