📚 給家長的話 · For Parents

🎯 學習重點 · Learning Focus

看著格子圖、記住物品位置、再拖回正確的格子 — 訓練短期記憶、空間位置感與專注力。不少小一面試都有「看圖記憶」環節：給你看幾十秒圖畫，收起之後問細節。這個遊戲正是針對式練習，還分了3×3與3×4兩種難度。

Look at the grid, remember where everything is, and drag each item back to its cell — training short-term memory, spatial awareness, and focus. Many Primary One interviews include a "memorise the picture" segment: study a picture for a few dozen seconds, then answer details after it's hidden. This game is targeted practice, with 3×3 and 3×4 difficulties.

👨‍👩‍👧 陪玩小貼士 · Playing Together

教小朋友用「講故事」策略：不要死記，用說話說出位置 — 「車車在左上角、魚魚在中間」。把位置說出口，記憶會穩固很多。由3×3三種物品開始，熟練之後才挑戰有相似物品的3×4。

Teach the "tell a story" strategy: don't memorise blindly — say the positions out loud: "the car is top-left, the fish is in the middle." Verbalising positions strengthens memory. Start with 3×3 and three items; move to 3×4 with similar-looking items once confident.

🏠 生活延伸練習 · Everyday Practice

家中實物版：把4-5樣物品放在托盤，讓小朋友看10秒，蓋住、拿走一樣，問「少了哪樣？」簡單但非常有效的記憶訓練，等位、搭車都玩得到。

A home version: place 4–5 objects on a tray, show your child for 10 seconds, cover it, remove one, and ask "what's missing?" Simple but highly effective memory training — playable while waiting or travelling.
