📚 給家長的話 · For Parents

🎯 學習重點 · Learning Focus

看著格子圖、記住物品位置、再拖回正確的格子 — 訓練短期記憶、空間位置感與專注力。不少小一面試都有「看圖記憶」環節：給你看幾十秒圖畫，收起之後問細節。這個遊戲正是針對式練習，除了3×3與3×4兩種難度，還有第三關「對調偵探」：先記住一排圖案的順序（第一位、第二位……），圖案收起再出現時，其中兩個對調了位置，小朋友要找出是哪兩個 — 訓練順序記憶與專注觀察，還自然練習到序數（第幾位）的概念。

Look at the grid, remember where everything is, and drag each item back to its cell — training short-term memory, spatial awareness, and focus. Many Primary One interviews include a "memorise the picture" segment: study a picture for a few dozen seconds, then answer details after it's hidden. This game is targeted practice with 3×3 and 3×4 difficulties, plus Sublevel 3 "Swap Detective": memorise the order of a row of icons (1st, 2nd…); the row hides and returns with two icons swapped, and your child must find which two — training sequential memory, close observation, and ordinal numbers.

👨‍👩‍👧 陪玩小貼士 · Playing Together

教小朋友用「講故事」策略：不要死記，用說話說出位置 — 「車車在左上角、魚魚在中間」。把位置說出口，記憶會穩固很多。由3×3三種物品開始，熟練之後才挑戰有相似物品的3×4。玩「對調偵探」時，鼓勵小朋友把順序讀出聲：「第一位是大象、第二位是斑馬……」把位置說出口，比默默看記得牢固得多。

Teach the "tell a story" strategy: don't memorise blindly — say the positions out loud: "the car is top-left, the fish is in the middle." Verbalising positions strengthens memory. Start with 3×3 and three items; move to 3×4 with similar-looking items once confident. For Swap Detective, encourage your child to read the row aloud: "first is the elephant, second is the zebra…" — saying it out loud sticks far better than silent looking.

🏠 生活延伸練習 · Everyday Practice

家中實物版：把4-5樣物品放在托盤，讓小朋友看10秒，蓋住、拿走一樣，問「少了哪樣？」簡單但非常有效的記憶訓練，等位、搭車都玩得到。

A home version: place 4–5 objects on a tray, show your child for 10 seconds, cover it, remove one, and ask "what's missing?" Simple but highly effective memory training — playable while waiting or travelling.
