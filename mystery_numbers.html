📚 給家長的話 · For Parents

🎯 學習重點 · Learning Focus

每幅圖畫都藏著一個數字：🍎+🍎=10，那麼🍎是多少？圖畫方程式其實是兒童版代數 — 用符號代表未知數、逐步推理、代入驗證。這類題目在小一面試屬於拉分題，考的不是計算速度，而是邏輯思考。

Every picture hides a number: 🍎+🍎=10, so 🍎=? Picture equations are algebra for children — symbols for unknowns, step-by-step reasoning, substitution to check. In Primary One interviews these are the differentiating questions, testing logic rather than speed.

👨‍👩‍👧 陪玩小貼士 · Playing Together

由「兩個相同圖畫」入手最容易：「兩個一樣的加起來是10，一個是多少？」熟練後才挑戰有不同圖畫的組合。最重要的是：忍住不要說答案 — 讓小朋友自己想出來，「想」的過程才是訓練。

Start with "two identical pictures": "two of the same add up to 10 — what is one?" Move to mixed pictures only when ready. Most important: resist giving the answer — let your child work it out; the thinking is the training.

🏠 生活延伸練習 · Everyday Practice

自己動手畫！用動物貼紙或者撲克牌自製圖畫謎題考小朋友；角色調換、由他出題你猜，他會理解得更加深。

Make your own! Use animal stickers or playing cards to create picture puzzles for your child; swap roles so they set the puzzle and you guess — understanding deepens when they create.
