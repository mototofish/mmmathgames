📚 給家長的話 · For Parents

🎯 學習重點 · Learning Focus

左右概念看似簡單，卻是小一面試和校內評估的常見失分位。關鍵在於「觀點轉換」：背向我們的人，左右和我們相同；面向我們的人，左右和我們相反。第二關更進一步，要求孩子想像自己置身圖中，模擬主角轉向後看到的景象 — 這種「心智旋轉」正是空間想像力的核心，也是日後學習方向、座標與幾何的基礎。

Left and right seem simple, yet they are a classic stumbling block in Primary One interviews and assessments. The key skill is perspective-taking: a person with their back to us shares our left and right, while a person facing us is mirrored. The second sublevel goes further — children must imagine themselves inside the picture and mentally rotate with the character. This "mental rotation" is the heart of spatial imagination and the foundation for directions, coordinates and geometry later on.

👨‍👩‍👧 陪玩小貼士 · Playing Together

先用身體學習：和孩子面對面玩「跟我做」— 你舉左手，孩子要舉起和你同一邊的手，他自然會發現面向你的人左右是相反的。答錯時不用急著糾正，請孩子站起來，親自面向圖中主角的方向，再慢慢轉一次。也可以在孩子右手背貼一張小貼紙作為標記，熟練之後才把貼紙拿掉。

Learn with the body first: play "copy me" face to face — you raise your left hand and your child raises the hand on the same side; they will discover for themselves that someone facing you is mirrored. When an answer is wrong, don't rush to correct it — ask your child to stand up, face the same way as the character, and turn slowly. A small sticker on the back of the right hand works wonders as a marker until it is no longer needed.

🏠 生活延伸練習 · Everyday Practice

生活處處是方向練習：過馬路時「向左望、向右望」；收拾玩具時「把車車放進左邊的箱子」；跳舞做體操時聽口令轉向。煮飯時也可以問：「你面向雪櫃，向左轉會看到什麼？」每天幾句方向口令，左右概念自然內化。

Direction practice is everywhere: "look left, look right" before crossing the road; "put the cars in the box on the left" at tidy-up time; turning to commands during dance or PE. Even while cooking: "face the fridge — what would you see if you turned left?" A few direction prompts a day and left/right becomes second nature.
