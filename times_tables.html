📚 給家長的話 · For Parents

🎯 學習重點 · Learning Focus

2、4、6、8、10！跳著數（skip counting）是乘法的熱身：「9個2相加是多少？」其實已經是 2×9 的意思，只是還未寫成乘式。遊戲用一籃籃物件，讓小朋友親眼見到「幾個幾」的結構 — 理解先於背誦，乘數表之後才背得有意思、記得牢。

2, 4, 6, 8, 10! Skip counting is the warm-up for multiplication: "nine 2s added together make…?" is already 2×9 in meaning, just not yet written as a multiplication. Counting baskets of objects lets children see the "groups of" structure — understanding before memorising, so times tables stick with meaning.

👨‍👩‍👧 陪玩小貼士 · Playing Together

一起拍手跳數：2、4、6、8… 拍一下說一個，節奏感對記憶幫助很大。見到「9籃、每籃2個」，引導小朋友說出「9個2」— 語言與算式接軌，是乘法概念的關鍵一步。

Clap and skip-count together: 2, 4, 6, 8… one clap per number — rhythm helps memory enormously. Seeing "9 baskets of 2", guide your child to say "nine 2s" — connecting language to the equation is the key step into multiplication.

🏠 生活延伸練習 · Everyday Practice

數筷子（一對對，2個一數）、雞蛋盒（一盒10隻）、紐扣、手指（每人10隻）— 家裡周圍都是「幾個幾」的現成素材，隨時可以玩。

Count chopsticks (pairs — count by 2s), egg cartons (10 a box), buttons, fingers (10 per person) — "groups of" materials are all over the house, ready to play anytime.
