📚 給家長的話 · For Parents

🎯 學習重點 · Learning Focus

觀察天秤：沉下去的一邊重、升起的一邊輕、平衡就一樣重。遊戲逐步加入更多動物，訓練傳遞性推理：A比B重、B比C重，所以A比C重 — 不用磅也推論到答案。這種邏輯是小一面試高階題的常見套路，考觀察，更考推理。

Read the balance scale: the sinking side is heavier, the rising side lighter, level means equal. More animals join gradually, training transitive reasoning: A is heavier than B, B is heavier than C, so A is heavier than C — no weighing needed. This logic appears in harder Primary One interview questions, testing observation and reasoning alike.

👨‍👩‍👧 陪玩小貼士 · Playing Together

教小朋友用完整句子作答：「獅子比猴子重。」而不是只指著圖畫。遇到三隻動物比較時，引導他逐對逐對地想，不要一口氣亂猜 — 有步驟的推理才是目標。

Teach your child to answer in full sentences: "The lion is heavier than the monkey" — not just pointing. With three animals, guide them to reason pair by pair instead of guessing all at once — step-by-step reasoning is the goal.

🏠 生活延伸練習 · Everyday Practice

廚房磅是好教具：磅水果、磅水杯，先估後磅。沒有磅的話，用雙手做「人肉天秤」，手掌一上一下模仿傾斜，小朋友會玩得非常開心。

A kitchen scale is a great teaching aid: weigh fruit and cups — guess first, then weigh. No scale? Use both hands as a human balance, tipping palms up and down — children love it.
