🎯 學習重點 · Learning Focus

動物與房屋亂成一團！「一對一」環節用一一對應比較多少：每隻動物配一間屋，一眼看到哪樣多、多多少。「兩隻一屋」就是除法與餘數的前奏：兩隻住一間，有多少隻沒有屋子住？有多少間空屋？全部都是小一面試的常見圖畫題。

Animals and houses all jumbled up! The "one-to-one" stage compares quantities by matching: one animal per house shows at a glance which is more, and by how much. "Two animals per house" is the prelude to division with remainders: how many are left homeless? How many houses stay empty? All common picture questions in Primary One interviews.

👨‍👩‍👧 陪玩小貼士 · Playing Together

教小朋友用筆（或者手指）逐對連線、圈一圈 — 「一隻對一間」，不要用眼估。答「多多少」時數出聲：「多了1、2、3 — 多3間。」步驟分明，不會亂。

Teach your child to draw matching lines or circle pairs with a pen (or finger) — "one animal to one house", not guessing by eye. When answering "how many more", count out loud: "1, 2, 3 — 3 more." Clear steps, no confusion.

🏠 生活延伸練習 · Everyday Practice

收襪子配對、把鞋子擺成一對對、「兩個人一張椅子，6個人需要多少張椅子？」— 配對思想在家裡隨處可以練，不用額外買教具。

Pair up socks, line shoes up in pairs, "two people per chair — how many chairs for 6 people?" — matching ideas can be practised anywhere at home, no special materials needed.
